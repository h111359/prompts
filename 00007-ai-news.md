## 1. Purpose

Produce AI daily briefing. 

## 2. Context

The user has FOMO and wants to be updated with everything important happening in AI

## 3. Objectives

- No major news are missed
- No obsolete information is included
- Format is followed strictly
- The user can read in 5 min
- At execution determine the current time in Europe/Sofia and research the preceding 24 hours using fresh live internet research. 

## 4. Scope and boundaries

- Exclude unsupported news and recycled announcements. 

- Score and rank 5–10 meaningful developments /25 (impact, novelty, practical value, evidence strength, strategic relevance), with verified dates, primary links, concise technical evidence, significance, applications and recommended actions. 

- If fewer than five qualify, add a clearly separate section with up to five important developments from the preceding seven days, labeled with original dates. 

## 5. Sources and evidence

- Monitor OpenAI, Anthropic, Google/DeepMind, Microsoft/GitHub, Meta and xAI/X and all six domains: 
    - frontier models; 
    - prompt/context engineering; 
    - agents and automation; 
    - vibe coding and AI development; 
    - research/open source; 
    - enterprise AI. 

## 6. Rules

- Prioritize original announcements, official changelogs, documentation, original research and repositories; 

- Seek independent verification; distinguish Confirmed, Vendor-reported, Independently evaluated and Unverified; 

- State report generation time, precise reporting windows and Europe/Sofia timezone; respect daylight saving.

Target 1,000–1,500 analytical words or 5 minutes reading effort; extend only for exceptional developments. 

- Include direct citations, do not invent links, dates, benchmarks or announcements; disclose research limitations. 

- Do not rely on previous briefings.

## 7. Response requirements

Include the following sections: 
1. Executive summary
Summarise in <=200 words; 

2. Latest News
Ranked last-24-hour news table and analysis; 

3. Last week
Conditional previous-seven-days context; 

4. Insights

Search for usefull content showing technical intelligence with comparable verified benchmarks and caveats;

5. Techniques

Pick a topic from areas of prompt engineering, agents and vibe coding with reusable prompt/workflow and present it shortly with its latest developments; 

6. Ideas 

Search latest publications of business needs and personal productivity or usage problems and generate three suggestions how to use AI to resolve these problems 

7. Strategic outlook 
Find three strategic tendencies, forecasts, researches.
